01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

I work at the intersection of software engineering and artificial intelligence, with a particular interest in how we can test, evaluate and explain machine-learning systems.

I am a Profesor Ayudante Doctor at the Universidad Complutense de Madrid (UCM), where I teach programming in the Data Science degree and contribute to research in software quality and artificial intelligence.

My research combines empirical evaluation, testing techniques and interpretable models to better understand when machine-learning systems behave as expected—and when they do not.

02 / RESEARCH

Research directions.

Methods and tools for making AI systems more robust, transparent and dependable.

01

Metamorphic Testing

Testing machine-learning systems when a complete test oracle is unavailable, using relations between inputs and expected outputs to reveal unexpected behaviour.

Software testing · Robustness · Test oracles
02
◇

Explainable AI

Developing and evaluating ways to describe feature contributions and interactions in predictive models, including tree-based methods.

XAI · Feature interactions · Interpretability
03
⌁

Anomaly Detection

Studying the behaviour and reliability of unsupervised methods under data transformations and changing conditions.

Unsupervised learning · Evaluation · Stability
04
⌘

Trustworthy Machine Learning

Empirical assessment of data-driven systems, including robustness, data quality, imputation and applications in cybersecurity.

Data quality · Reliability · Cybersecurity
